Six sessions on the craft of teaching

The idea

Not another certificate.

Most teacher training hands you an anatomy syllabus and an exam. This is about the part nobody teaches you.

How a class is actually built. Where you stand and what your voice is doing. How to look at a body and know what it is telling you. How to choose the right cue for the person in front of you, under pressure, in the moment. And then teaching, watched and corrected, until it holds.

It is led by Denitsa, who has been teaching movement for over a decade, and it runs in small groups so that everyone actually gets taught.

The programme

Six sessions, in order.

Each one builds on the last, and the sixth is the one the others were for.

01

The art of the class

A class is not a list of exercises. It is a shape someone is taken through, and that shape is what they feel afterwards. Most teachers build from what they like teaching. This is about noticing that, then choosing.

02

The teacher's presence

Where you stand, what your voice does, when you demonstrate and when you say nothing. The part of teaching that is not the content, and decides whether the content lands at all.

03

Learning to see, part one

Seeing is a skill, not a talent. The lower half first: what the spine, pelvis, hips and legs are actually doing, and how you know when they are not.

04

Learning to see, part two

The upper half, then the whole. This one ends on integration, reading a body as one thing rather than a stack of separate regions.

05

The art of cueing

Three channels: what you say, what you show, what you touch. Most teachers rely on one and default to it under pressure. This is about choosing, in the moment, the channel the person in front of you actually needs.

06

The apprenticeship

You teach. Everything from the first five sessions, used on real bodies, watched and corrected. The session the other five were building towards.

Who it is for

Teachers who want to teach better.

You are already teaching, or you have finished a certification and feel it did not prepare you for a real room. You want to be watched by someone who will tell you the truth, and you are willing to teach in front of other teachers and be corrected.

You do not need years behind you. You do need to want this more than you want another certificate for the wall.
