Activity

Surfing

Ahangama has a reef break that works for beginners and improves fast. Boards and lessons are available right on the beach, and most people pick it up within a day or two. A good counterbalance to the reformer.

Activity

Yoga

Mat yoga classes run at the studio alongside Pilates. If you want something slower, more breath-led and meditative to balance your reformer days, this is it.

Activity

Cooking class

Sri Lankan food is genuinely one of the best things about being here. A local cooking class will teach you rice and curry from scratch. The kind you'll spend years trying to recreate at home.

Activity

Day trips

Galle Fort is 20 minutes away. Mirissa for whale watching, Udawalawe for elephants, and Ella for hills and waterfalls are all doable from Ahangama. Good drivers are easy to find and not expensive.

Activity

Ahangama town pass

A discount card that gets you savings across local cafes, restaurants and activities around Ahangama. We can sort it for you. $30, and it pays for itself fast.

All optional. Add any of them when you enquire, or decide once you are here. Nothing is bundled in without you asking.
