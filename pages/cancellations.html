Plans change. Here's how to cancel or move your class in a few clicks.

There are two simple guides below. Use the first one if your class is more than 72 hours away and you want a refund. Use the second one if your class is sooner than that and you want to move to another class. If you get stuck at any point, message us on WhatsApp and we'll sort it for you.

Want to cancel and get a refund?

If your class is more than 72 hours away, you can cancel it yourself and get a refund to your original payment method. A small 5% booking fee is kept. Here is how to do it:

Step 1. Log in, then open the account menu in the top right and click "Customer portal".

Less than 72 hours before your class?

You cannot get a refund this close to your class, but you can move it to a different class for free. You can transfer up until 12 hours before your original class, and the new class must be within 1 week.

Step 1. Open "Options" on your class and click "Transfer ticket". The "Cancel" option will be greyed out.
