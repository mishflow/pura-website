The programme

Not a certification.
A confidence builder.

You leave able to run a room, not just pass a test.

This is not a certification course. It is a teacher improvement programme, run from our boutique studio on Sri Lanka's south coast, for people who already practise Pilates and want to get genuinely good at teaching it. You sharpen the things that actually matter in front of a class: cueing, programming, reading a body, and the mat and reformer repertoire. The setting is part of it. Ahangama is small, the ocean is close, and the pace lets you absorb what you are learning instead of cramming it.

From the first week you are on the reformer and on your feet. You observe real classes, then teach them. You work with real clients, not just your fellow trainees, under the eye of instructors who teach here every day. Feedback is direct and immediate. Cohorts are deliberately small, so nobody hides at the back and nobody waits their turn for a machine. The point is simple. Come, enjoy the culture, and walk out far more confident standing in front of a room.

You will not leave with a piece of paper to hang on a wall. You will leave with the harder thing to find: the hours, the instinct, and the confidence to teach a class that holds.

What sets it apart

Built for real teachers.

⏱
Hands-on hours from day one
You teach real classes early and often, not in a single assessment at the end. Most of your training is spent coaching live bodies, building the instinct that only comes from reps.
◯
Small cohorts
Places are capped so every trainee gets individual attention and direct mentorship. No crowds, no waiting your turn, no falling through the cracks.
▭
Full reformer access
A machine to yourself for the whole course. You practise, cue, and troubleshoot the reformer repertoire until it is second nature, the way a working teacher needs it to be.
◈
Trained in Sri Lanka
Ahangama is the setting, not the backdrop. A focused studio on the south coast, working instructors who teach here daily, and the space to learn properly between sessions in the water and the sun.
